- **[5] Orland:** conference abstract (ASH 2025), not peer-reviewed. Labelled "conference abstract" at every first body appearance (×6 in the manuscript). Keep the label.
- **[33]–[35] (new §5c, 2026-10-07):** first-appearance order is [33] (STEP-CRP), [34] (Masson meta-analysis), [35] (Miertová submaximal-dose). All three are in people with overweight/obesity, cardiovascular disease or type 2 diabetes; **none enrolled normal-weight, non-diabetic adults.** In §5c this is stated plainly as the inferential, unoccupied node the Section-10 design tests (the normal-weight extrapolation). Miertová [35] measured weight/waist, **not** inflammatory biomarkers — cite it only as evidence that submaximal dosing is pharmacologically active and tolerated in real-world use, not as an anti-inflammatory effect. Verma [33] is Novo Nordisk-funded; Masson [34] reports some Novo Nordisk speaker fees — grade both as hypothesis-generating where the mechanism claim is made, not as independent proof.
